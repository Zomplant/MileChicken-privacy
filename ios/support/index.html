MileChicken

iPhone and iPad support

Contact us

Email MileChicken@mail.com with your device model, app version, and a description of what happened. For a missed drive, include the date and approximate departure time. You can leave out the addresses and GPS route.

Record a drive

Turn on automatic drive detection on the Dashboard. For background tracking, set location access to Always and enable Precise Location. You can also tap the plus button to enter a missed trip.

Trip allowance and subscriptions

The free plan includes 40 trips per month. Check Settings to see your remaining trips or view Unlimited plans. To recover an existing Apple subscription, open Plans and choose Restore purchases using the Apple Account you used to subscribe.
