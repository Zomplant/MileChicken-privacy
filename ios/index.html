MileChicken is a mileage-record app offered under the Zomplant Apps business name. This policy covers MileChicken for iOS. For privacy questions or support, contact MileChicken@mail.com.

Trip records and settings

MileChicken stores trip dates and times, mileage, classifications, saved route points when present, and app settings in the app's local storage on your device. It uses these records to display trip history, calculate mileage totals, and apply the monthly new-trip allowance. No MileChicken account is required. This version does not provide a MileChicken cloud-sync service or automatically upload your trip records to Zomplant Apps.

Location and other features

If you enable automatic drive tracking, MileChicken requests location permission and uses precise GPS locations, timestamps, accuracy, and speed to detect drives and record mileage and routes. Tracking continues in the background while enabled and permitted by iOS, with the system location indicator. Location and trip records stay on this device; they are not automatically uploaded to Zomplant Apps. Manual trips work without location permission.

You can stop tracking with the automatic drive detection switch or revoke location access in iPhone Settings. Force-quitting the app stops tracking until you reopen it. Turning tracking off discards the unfinished drive. An interrupted route may be kept in private app storage so it can be recovered when the app runs again.

If you enable trip notifications, iOS asks for permission to show them. You can change that permission in Settings.

Optional online map

The trip page displays a local route preview. If you tap the preview or choose Open Map, MileChicken opens an online map using the OpenStreetMap Foundation's tile service. Your device requests map images for the area and zoom level shown. These requests can reveal detailed places along a saved trip, even though the complete saved route is drawn locally and is not included in the tile request URL. Viewing an online map is optional and is not required to record or save a trip.

OpenStreetMap and its service providers receive network and request information, which can include your IP address, browser or app and device information, operating system, application identification, request times, and requested map images. The provider uses service records for operation, security, planning, and aggregate usage analysis. It also publishes anonymized summary statistics about requested map images and apps or websites using the service. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy and tile-service policy.

Zomplant Apps does not operate the map tile service or control its service records. Deleting a trip from MileChicken does not delete records already held by an external service. Map images may also be cached on your device.

While a map is open, MileChicken creates a temporary local document containing its route. The document is removed when the map closes. An interrupted app session may leave a copy in private temporary storage until it is cleared.

App Store subscriptions

If you choose a paid Unlimited plan, Apple handles payments, renewals, cancellations, and refunds through the App Store. MileChicken uses Apple’s StoreKit service to display subscription prices and check your verified subscription status.

Apple provides the app with purchase information, including the product, subscription dates, and renewal or revocation status, so MileChicken can enable or remove Unlimited access. These billing checks do not include your trip records or location history.

MileChicken does not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account password. This iOS version does not send purchase receipts or subscription status to a Zomplant Apps server. Apple maintains purchase records under its own privacy practices. See Apple’s App Store privacy information.

You can restore an existing subscription using the Apple Account you subscribed with. Deleting trips or removing MileChicken does not cancel an App Store subscription. You can manage or cancel renewal through your Apple Account’s subscription settings.

Support and external services

If you contact support, the information you choose to include in your email is handled by the email services involved and is available to us so we can respond. Send only information needed to explain your request. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. Opening an external link uses a separate app or website, whose privacy practices apply to that interaction.

MileChicken does not use your trip records for advertising. The app does not include advertising or a separate analytics SDK. OpenStreetMap's service logging and analysis are described above.

Retention and deletion

Trip records remain in local storage until you delete them or remove the app and its data. You can delete an individual trip from Trip Detail or by pressing and holding a trip in Trips, then confirming deletion. Settings also provides trip deletion for a date range or for all trips. Deleting trips does not restore used monthly allowance. App settings and quota records may remain after trip deletion.

If you enable automatic cleanup, records older than two years are deleted when the app starts. Removing the app and its data removes its local records, but may not remove copies held in device backups or information previously sent through another service.

Reports and sharing

When you export a report, you choose whether to save the CSV or spreadsheet file in Files or share it using the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination or recipient. Reports can include dates, mileage, classification, trip times, a trip identifier, and start or end coordinates for GPS trips. The chosen app or storage provider handles the copy under its own privacy practices. MileChicken does not automatically upload reports to Zomplant Apps.

Report password protection is optional. When selected, the report is placed in an AES-encrypted ZIP file before saving or sharing. The archive's contents require the password, although its filename and file sizes can remain visible. MileChicken does not save, send, or recover the password. Give the password to recipients separately; they need software that supports AES-encrypted ZIP files. Unencrypted reports and copies extracted by recipients can be read by anyone with access to them. Creating an encrypted report does not protect earlier copies.

Backup and restore

A trip backup contains dates and times, exact distances, classifications, notes, and full saved GPS routes. When you create a backup, you choose where to save the file in Files. A storage provider you select may upload or retain it under its own privacy practices. MileChicken does not automatically upload backups to Zomplant Apps.

Backup password protection is optional and selected by default. If enabled, the file is encrypted using the password you choose. If disabled, anyone with access to the file can read its trip and location history. MileChicken does not save or send the password and cannot recover it. Keep both the file and password. Creating an encrypted backup does not encrypt or delete older unencrypted copies.

To restore trips, you choose a MileChicken backup in Files, review the preview, and confirm. MileChicken adds new trips to the device and keeps existing trips. Backups do not include subscription purchase tokens, phone permissions, or app settings. An App Store subscription is restored separately through Apple. Backup protection applies to the backup file, not the app's local database or separately saved reports.

Temporary working files are removed after an operation; an interrupted operation may leave a copy in private temporary storage until cleared. Working files for password-protected backups are encrypted. Working copies of unencrypted backups are not encrypted.

Device backups and security

Apple device backups or device transfers may include app data depending on your device and Apple Account settings. Apple handles those services under its own privacy practices and your settings. You can also create or restore a trip backup yourself in MileChicken.

MileChicken uses the device's local app storage. The app does not provide a separate password-encrypted trip database or a service to recover deleted records. Keep your device secured and review your device-backup settings.
