MileChicken is a mileage-record app offered under the Zomplant Apps business name. This policy covers the current manual-trip iOS version. For privacy questions or support, contact MileChicken@mail.com.

Trip records and settings

MileChicken stores trip dates and times, mileage, classifications, and app settings in the app's local storage on your device. It uses these records to display trip history, calculate mileage totals, and apply the monthly new-trip allowance. No MileChicken account is required. This version does not provide a MileChicken cloud-sync service or automatically upload your trip records to Zomplant Apps.

Location and other features

The current iOS version does not implement automatic drive detection, GPS recording, online route maps, drive notifications, report exporting or sharing, or backup file selection. It does not request location or notification permission. The Android app's Google Play billing, location recording, maps, exports, and backup features do not apply to this iOS version.

App Store subscriptions

If you choose a paid Unlimited plan, Apple handles payments, renewals, cancellations, and refunds through the App Store. MileChicken uses Apple’s StoreKit service to display subscription prices and check your verified subscription status.

Apple provides the app with purchase information, including the product, subscription dates, and renewal or revocation status, so MileChicken can enable or remove Unlimited access. These billing checks do not include your trip records or location history.

MileChicken does not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account password. This iOS version does not send purchase receipts or subscription status to a Zomplant Apps server. Apple maintains purchase records under its own privacy practices. See Apple’s App Store privacy information.

You can restore an existing subscription using the Apple Account you subscribed with. Deleting trips or removing MileChicken does not cancel an App Store subscription. You can manage or cancel renewal through your Apple Account’s subscription settings.

Support and external services

If you contact support, the information you choose to include in your email is handled by the email services involved and is available to us so we can respond. Send only information needed to explain your request. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. Opening an external link uses a separate app or website, whose privacy practices apply to that interaction.

MileChicken does not use your trip records for advertising. The current iOS app does not include advertising or an analytics integration.

Retention and deletion

Trip records remain in local storage until you delete them or remove the app and its data. You can delete an individual trip from Trip Detail or by pressing and holding a trip in Trips, then confirming deletion. Settings also provides trip deletion for a date range or for all trips. Deleting trips does not restore used monthly allowance. App settings and quota records may remain after trip deletion.

If you enable automatic cleanup, records older than two years are deleted when the app starts. Removing the app and its data removes its local records, but may not remove copies held in device backups or information previously sent through another service.

Device backups and security

Apple device backups or device transfers may include app data depending on your device and Apple Account settings. Those services are governed by Apple's privacy practices and your settings. MileChicken does not provide its own backup or restore feature in this iOS version.

MileChicken uses the device's local app storage. The app does not provide a separate password-encrypted trip database or a service to recover deleted records. Keep your device secured and review your device-backup settings.

This policy website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub's privacy practices apply to visits to this website. Visiting this page does not upload your local MileChicken trip database. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the iOS app's features or data handling change. The updated policy and date will be posted here. Contact MileChicken@mail.com with privacy, support, or deletion questions.
